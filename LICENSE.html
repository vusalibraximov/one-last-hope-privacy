# ONE LAST HOPE — Privacy Policy **Effective Date: November 25, 2026** ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how ONE LAST HOPE ("the Game"), developed by the developer of ONE LAST HOPE, handles information when you use the mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. ## 2. Information We Collect The Game does not require users to create an account to play. However, certain third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically collect information, including: - Device information and device identifiers. - IP address and network information. - Advertising identifiers. - Advertising interactions and preferences. - App performance and diagnostic information. The exact information collected depends on the services and features being used. ## 3. Advertising — Google AdMob The Game may use Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements. Advertisements are available only in Single Player mode and may be offered after the player reaches the Game Over screen. Players can choose whether to watch an advertisement. Successfully completing the rewarded advertisement grants double Null Coins for the eligible run reward. No advertisements are displayed in Multiplayer mode. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic data to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and support advertising services. For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 4. Multiplayer and Network Information The Game includes online Multiplayer functionality. When using Multiplayer features, certain network information, such as IP addresses and connection information, may be processed to establish and maintain peer-to-peer connections. Third-party networking services may be used to facilitate connections between players. Players should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information through multiplayer nicknames or other in-game communication features. ## 5.
